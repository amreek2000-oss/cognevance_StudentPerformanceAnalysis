## 📌 Executive Summary
An in-depth **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** conducted on a student performance dataset to uncover key factors influencing academic success. The primary objective is to evaluate the impact of **attendance rates**, **internal assessments**, and **study habits** on final exam performance.

## 📊 Key Findings & Insights
1. **Attendance Impact**: High attendance students (>85%) achieved significantly higher mean scores compared to low attendance students (<75%).
2. **Pearson Correlation**: Strong positive linear correlation ($r \approx 0.72$, $p < 0.001$) confirmed between attendance and final exam performance.
3. **Performance Gap**: Students maintaining regular attendance scored **25+ marks higher** on average than low-attendance peers.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. 🚨 **Early Warning System**: Implement automated alerts when attendance drops below **80%**.
2. 📚 **Targeted Remedial Support**: Mandatory academic intervention for low-performing students (<50 marks).
3. 📋 **Policy Threshold**: Enforce a minimum **75% attendance criterion** for final exam eligibility.
